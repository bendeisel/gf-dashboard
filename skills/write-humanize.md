Invoke the `avoid-ai-writing` skill (Skill tool) first, then work on the draft below.

Default to a rewrite that keeps the meaning and the author's voice but removes the tells (filler openers, triplets, hedging, generic sign-offs, "delve"-style vocabulary). Show the result, then a short list of what you changed and why. For Growth Factor-facing copy, the voice is direct and practical — we automate things, we don't hype them. If the draft is short, give me two versions: tight and friendlier.
